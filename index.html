Heritage & Trust

Attur's Trusted Heritage for Furniture & Home Electronics

Located on Ammampalayam in Attur, Salem, Ganapathy & Co has stood as a cornerstone of reliability for generations of homeowners. Our store was founded with a singular commitment: to deliver sturdy, finely crafted solid teak furniture and trusted brand-name electronics at honest, transparent prices.

Whether you are setting up a new home, upgrading your living space with energy-efficient appliances, or looking for durable cots, wardrobes, and dining sets, our store brings together quality and traditional craftsmanship under one single roof.
